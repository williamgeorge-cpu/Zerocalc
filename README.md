My first project for the TI 84 Plus CE!
So I am on the math team for my school and doing precalc and whatnot and wanted a faster way to find the zeros of a quadratic equation than the traditional ti 84 Plus CE way or a 
stupid flippin TI BASIC program, because those are for bums. Unfortunately most of this was in fact generated by LLMs (Gemini) and I had to go in and make it actually work, because
apparently Gemini can't do that. I also manually matched the colors to what they were supposed to be, and you can pretty easily figure out what it's based off of. It was made in C
(SourceCoder 3) and I've provided the source code and compiled program. Like all calc programs just download the .8xp and send it to your calc with WebTILP or ticalc.link.
